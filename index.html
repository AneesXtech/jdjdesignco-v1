FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need a consultation before hiring you?

No, but most people start there. It is the fastest way to find out whether your idea is manufacturable before committing to a design budget, and it is credited back if you go on to commission a project.

Should I do OEM branding or a custom device?

If a device already on the market would work with your branding on it, brand it — you are to market in about forty days instead of a year, for a fraction of the cost. Go custom when the thing you need does not exist yet, or when owning the design is the point.

How long does a custom device really take?

Six to twelve months from concept to market-ready, sometimes longer. Design is thirty days of that; the rest is tooling, validation, and production, and those lead times are not compressible. OEM branding is the answer if you need product sooner.

Who owns the design?

You do. Once final payment clears and the files are transferred, the design is yours outright — 100%. I retain no equity and no ownership stake unless we agree otherwise in writing beforehand.

Can you manufacture a design I already own?

Yes. Bring your files and I will review them for manufacturability, then route production through the right partner factory.

What do you need before packaging work starts?

Your color palette with Pantone (PMS) numbers, your logo files in vector format, and a written design direction. Without those three, the 30-business-day clock does not start.
